ASK OUR TEAM ↗
Where is Seranova Infra based?

Seranova Infra Private Limited is based in Mandur, Bengaluru, Karnataka. You can find our address and location map on the Contact page.

How do I request a construction quotation?

Fill in the quotation form at the top of this page with your name, mobile number and project type. Our team will contact you within 1 hour after submission to discuss your requirements.

What affects the cost of a construction project?

The quotation depends on your project location, design, site conditions, specifications, materials and individual requirements. Contact our team for a detailed quotation tailored to your project.

Where can I compare your construction packages?

Our Services page explains the package options. You can discuss the scope, finishes and specifications with our team before choosing an option.

Can I discuss renovation or interior work?

Yes. Select Renovation & Remodeling or Interior Design & Execution in the enquiry form. Our team will discuss your space, requirements and project scope.

What should I prepare for the first discussion?

Have your site location, approximate plot size, preferred project type and any drawings or ideas ready if available. These details help us understand what you want to build.
